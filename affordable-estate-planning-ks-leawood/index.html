Questions about fees and scope

Before moving ahead, ask how the firm handles fees and what services are included in the proposed work. The details can depend on your needs, so request an explanation you can refer to after the conversation. It’s reasonable to ask what might require additional work and how you’ll be told if the scope changes.

A useful discussion can cover questions such as:

  • Which planning concerns should we address first?
  • What work would be included in the proposed engagement?
  • How are fees explained and when will I learn about them?
  • What information do you need before discussing the work?
  • If my needs change, how will that affect the plan?

These questions aren’t about choosing the lowest number without context. They help you understand what you’re considering and whether it matches your goals.

Prepare before contacting a firm

Write down your main concerns in everyday language. Note whether you have a will or trust, whether you’re asking about probate, and what you hope a plan will accomplish. Gather existing paperwork if it’s easy to find, but don’t delay a call just because some records are missing. You can ask what information to collect next.

Try not to compare firms based only on a short description or a single fee figure without knowing what it covers. A document that doesn’t address your circumstances may leave important questions unresolved. It’s also unhelpful to postpone all planning while waiting for the perfect time to sort out every detail. Start with a conversation about the issue that matters most to you.
